# Skill vs Will Diagnosis Framework - Gong 2025-2026 Methodology

## Overview
The Skill vs Will diagnosis framework helps sales managers effectively coach representatives by distinguishing between skill gaps (can't do) and will gaps (won't do). This enables targeted coaching interventions based on the Gong methodology updated for 2025-2026.

## Skill Dimensions (Can't Do - Ability)
1. **Product Knowledge** - Understanding of product features, benefits, use cases, and competitive positioning
2. **Discovery** - Ability to uncover customer needs, pain points, and business impact through effective questioning
3. **Objection Handling** - Skill in addressing concerns and overcoming barriers to purchase
4. **Closing** - Proficiency in gaining commitment and moving opportunities forward
5. **Pipeline Management** - Competence in managing opportunities through the sales stages
6. **CRM Hygiene** - Ability to maintain accurate and timely data in the CRM system

## Will Dimensions (Won't Do - Motivation/Attitude)
1. **Effort** - Consistency and volume of activities (calls, emails, meetings)
2. **Resilience** - Ability to bounce back from rejection and maintain performance under pressure
3. **Coachability** - Openness to feedback and willingness to change behaviors
4. **Motivation** - Drive to achieve goals and commitment to personal/professional growth
5. **Time Management** - Effective prioritization and use of selling time

## 2x2 Matrix Placement

### Quadrant 1: High Skill / High Will (Stars)
- **Characteristics**: Top performers who consistently exceed targets
- **Coaching Focus**: Retention, growth, and leadership development
- **Monthly Cadence**: 
  - 1x strategic 1:1 (career development, advanced techniques)
  - 1x peer coaching session (sharing best practices)
  - Quarterly: Advanced skill workshops
- **Behavior Change Tracking**: One advanced skill refinement per quarter
- **Forecasting Integration**: Use as benchmarks in multivariable models; analyze their successful patterns for signal weighting

### Quadrant 2: High Skill / Low Will (Coach)
- **Characteristics**: Capable but inconsistent performers; may have attitude or motivation issues
- **Coaching Focus**: Motivation, accountability, and re-engagement
- **Monthly Cadence**:
  - 2x tactical 1:1s (activity review, obstacle removal)
  - 1x motivation/alignment session
  - Weekly: Activity compliance check-ins
- **Behavior Change Tracking**: One will-focused change per month (e.g., activity consistency, attitude shift)
- **Forecasting Integration**: Adjust forecast weights downward for will-related risk factors; monitor for improvement signals

### Quadrant 3: Low Skill / High Will (Develop)
- **Characteristics**: Motivated and eager but lacking skills; high potential
- **Coaching Focus**: Skill development, training, and confidence building
- **Monthly Cadence**:
  - 2x skill-building 1:1s (practice, role-play, feedback)
  - 1x progress review session
  - Bi-weekly: Targeted skill drills
- **Behavior Change Tracking**: One skill improvement per month (measured via call scoring, milestone achievement)
- **Forecasting Integration**: Increase forecast confidence as skills develop; track skill acquisition as leading indicator

### Quadrant 4: Low Skill / Low Will (Exit)
- **Characteristics**: Low performers with limited potential for improvement
- **Coaching Focus**: Performance improvement plan or transition
- **Monthly Cadence**:
  - 2x performance review 1:1s (PIP tracking, consequences clear)
  - 1x skills assessment session
  - Weekly: Compliance verification
- **Behavior Change Tracking**: Binary compliance tracking (meeting minimum standards)
- **Forecasting Integration**: Apply significant downward forecast adjustment; exclude from confidence-weighted models unless PIP shows sustained improvement

## Behavior Change Tracking Methodology
- **One Change Per Cycle**: Each coaching cycle focuses on mastering ONE specific behavior change
- **Measurement**: 
  - Skill changes: Measured via call recording scores, milestone achievement, or conversion rate improvement
  - Will changes: Measured via activity compliance, self-assessment scores, or peer feedback
- **Tracking System**: 
  - Baseline measurement before intervention
  - Weekly check-ins during cycle
  - End-of-cycle validation
  - Documented in rep's coaching plan

## Integration with Multivariable Forecasting (300+ Signals)
- **Skill Signals**: 
  - Product knowledge scores from certification assessments
  - Discovery effectiveness (needs uncovered per call)
  - Objection handling success rate
  - Close rate by stage
  - Pipeline velocity metrics
  - CRM data completeness and timeliness scores
  
- **Will Signals**:
  - Activity volume vs. quota (calls, emails, meetings)
  - Response time to leads (speed-to-lead)
  - Call duration and conversation balance
  - Follow-up consistency
  - Goal attainment trends
  - Coachability metrics (feedback implementation rate)
  
- **Forecasting Adjustments**:
  - Stars: +10-15% confidence multiplier for committed deals
  - Coach: -5-10% confidence multiplier (will risk factor)
  - Develop: Dynamic confidence adjustment based on skill trajectory
  - Exit: -20-30% confidence multiplier or exclusion from weighted forecast
  
- **Signal Weighting**: 
  - Will signals weighted 40% in early-stage forecasting
  - Skill signals weighted 60% in late-stage forecasting
  - Combined impact increases forecast accuracy by 8-12% based on Gong 2025 benchmarks

## Implementation Process
1. **Assessment Phase** (Monthly):
   - Score each rep on 1-5 scale for each dimension
   - Calculate composite Skill and Will scores
   - Place in appropriate quadrant
   - Review against forecast accuracy and pipeline health

2. **Planning Phase**:
   - Develop individualized coaching plan per quadrant guidelines
   - Define ONE behavior change focus for the cycle
   - Establish measurement methodology

3. **Execution Phase**:
   - Execute coaching cadence as specified
   - Track behavior change progress weekly
   - Adjust approach based on leading indicators

4. **Review Phase** (End of Cycle):
   - Measure behavior change achievement
   - Update Skill/Will scores
   - Forecasting team receives updated signal weights
   - Plan next cycle

## Data Sources for Assessment
- CRM activity logs
- Call recording analysis (AI-scored)
- Pipeline stage conversion metrics
- Quota attainment data
- 360-feedback surveys
- Self-assessment questionnaires
- Manager observations

## Success Metrics
- Reduction in time to move reps between quadrants (especially Develop → Star)
- Improvement in forecast accuracy correlation with Skill/Will assessments
- Increase in percentage of reps in Star quadrant
- Reduction in coaching cycles needed for behavior change adoption
