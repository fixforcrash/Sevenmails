## Deep Web Search Per Project (Mandatory Standard)

For any task that involves **live or volatile facts** — current pricing, product/feature changes, client-specific configuration, regulator or vendor updates, or current best practice — the agent MUST perform a **deep web search scoped to the project** BEFORE finalizing the deliverable. Do not rely solely on cached SOP/Playbook knowledge.
- **Tool:** the CRW crawler (`crw_scrape` for pages, `crw_map` for site discovery). Use `crw_map` on the vendor/authority domain, then `crw_scrape` the most relevant pages. Fallback: Jina via shell redirection (see Web Access standard).
- **Depth:** follow the project's key entities (vendor docs, official changelogs, authoritative guides) — not a single query. Capture what changed vs the cached SOP.
- **When NOT required:** pure planning/archival deliverables where the SOP is already authoritative and the user explicitly scopes to vault-only (e.g. Knowledge Sync dry-runs).
- **Record:** note the sources checked in the deliverable (Sources section) and store a Mnemosyne memory of any material update.

## Live Web Refresh (2026-08-03)

- The 18 social media trends to shape your 2026 strategy (Hootsuite, Christina Newberry) — https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-trends/ — Discovery is now interest-led, not follower-led: algorithms weight micro-behaviors (hover time, rewatches, pauses), so follower count is effectively a vanity metric and audiences arrive via "snowballs" (repeated themes across sources) rather than self-driven "rabbit holes". AI tooling is table stakes (79% of social managers use it daily) but audiences reject "AI slop" — >30% of consumers are less likely to choose a brand whose ads are known to be AI-generated, and 91% of marketers say human involvement is critical. Social is now a search surface: Google began indexing public Instagram posts/profiles in July 2025, making social SEO and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) real distribution levers. LinkedIn is called out as being "in its creative era", and employee advocacy is framed as authenticity amplified. (verified live via CRW on 2026-08-03)
- Social Selling: Definition, Benefits & Tips for Sales Leaders (LinkedIn Sales Solutions) — https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/social-selling — Primary-source benchmark numbers for social selling: leaders with a high Social Selling Index (SSI) create 45% more opportunities, are 51% more likely to hit quota, and 78% of social sellers outsell peers who don't use social. Top performers (150%+ of quota) were more than twice as likely to have expanded their LinkedIn network in the past year (53% vs 25%). Four pillars: create a professional brand with value, find the right prospects, engage with insights, build trusted relationships. Documented case studies: Microsoft +38% sales productivity, PTC $4.5M in closed deals, Genesys +42% deal size. Explicit framing that social selling has widened into "virtual/digital selling" across the whole sales tech stack, not just social networks. (verified live via CRW on 2026-08-03)
- The 2025 Sprout Social Index™, Edition XX — https://sproutsocial.com/insights/index/ — Survey of 4,044 consumers, 900 social practitioners and 322 marketing leaders (fielded Sept 13–27, 2024 by Glimpse). Three findings that matter for method: (1) culture ≠ chasing trends — consumers want brands to understand the *context* of cultural moments rather than recreate every meme, so brands should build their own cultural touchstones; (2) AI proficiency is a lifeline against creative burnout and is the unlock for specialized social roles; (3) an executive trust gap exists — leaders claim to grasp social's impact but practitioners are skeptical, so social teams must tell data stories tying opportunity cost to metrics leadership already cares about. (verified live via CRW on 2026-08-03)

### Skill improvements adopted

1. **Stop optimizing for followers; optimize for interest signals.** Report and target hover time, rewatches, saves and shares over follower growth. Assume any given post reaches non-followers first, and write the first 3 seconds as a hook because that is what generates the hover signal.
2. **Build content per platform, never cross-post raw.** Audience composition differs enough per network that a single asset repurposed unmodified underperforms on all of them. Adapt hook, format and framing per platform.
3. **Treat AI as draft/iterate/resize infrastructure, never as the published voice.** Use AI for brainstorming, variant generation, resizing and rapid A/B testing; keep a human editing pass and deliberate "proof of humanity" (process shots, named people, behind-the-scenes, unpolished takes) in every post. Do not ship uncurated output — the penalty is brand-level, not post-level.
4. **Write social posts for search and answer engines.** Keyword-research captions, always add alt text and subtitles, and add a recurring content slot of short question-answer posts (question-based title, caption written as a direct answer) to capture AEO and Google's Short Videos surface.
5. **Run social selling against the SSI four pillars and measure with SSI-style leading indicators.** Establish expertise and share value before any outreach; qualify hard on fit rather than volume; use warm intros and pertinent, insight-led conversations rather than scripts. Track network expansion rate as a leading indicator — it separates 150%-of-quota sellers from average ones.
6. **Anchor every internal pitch in the executive trust gap.** When requesting resources, lead with opportunity cost and business metrics, not engagement metrics, because leadership skepticism (not budget) is the documented blocker.
7. **Engage culture by context, not by meme replication.** Before joining a moment, state what the moment means to our specific audience; if we can't, skip it and invest in a repeatable owned touchstone instead.
8. **Run social as a research engine.** Use listening and deliberate creative experiments to collect first-party data and detect sentiment micro-shifts, and feed those findings back into positioning and outreach messaging before the moment peaks.
9. **Prioritize employee advocacy on LinkedIn.** Individual employee accounts carry the authenticity signal brand accounts have lost, and LinkedIn's current creative push rewards it.

- LinkedIn B2B Marketing Blog (index) — https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog — Live index confirming LinkedIn's 2026 B2B editorial priorities: measurement/"bullspend" (vanity metrics vs. business impact, Mar 2026), events as demand engines not cost centers (Jul 2026), AI as marketing *infrastructure* rather than campaign (Adobe, Jul 2026), and Cannes Lions 2026 B2B takeaways. Used as the landing page to resolve real article URLs instead of guessing slugs. (verified live via CRW on 2026-08-05)
- The Credibility Code: How B2B Brands Win Trust that Converts — https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/research-and-insights/the-credibility-code-how-b2b-brands-win-trust-that-converts — LinkedIn B2B Institute + Ipsos research (Alejandro Garcia Medina, Aug 5 2026). Core thesis: B2B deals are lost to *indecision*, not competitors — 40% of deals die that way (Jolt Effect); avg purchase = 10 stakeholders / 272 days (Dreamdata LinkedIn Ads Benchmarks 2025). Buyers optimize for the most *defensible* choice, not the best one on paper. Introduces the **Credibility Stack**: four reinforcing voices — brand (narrative/thought leadership; 9 in 10 decision-makers more receptive to outreach after strong TL, Edelman-LinkedIn 2024), employees (collectively reach 12x the company follower base), customers (buyers 3x more likely to choose a recommended vendor over a cheaper one — LinkedIn x Bain, Principles of Buyability 2025), and creators (instant access to pre-built trust; the most actionable and most underinvested layer). Credibility compounds through reinforcement, not repetition. (verified live via CRW on 2026-08-05)

### Skill improvements adopted

1. **Stop optimizing posts for reach; optimize campaigns for a compounding Credibility Stack.** For every client/campaign I now audit all four voices — brand POV, employee advocacy, customer proof, creator partnerships — and flag the missing layer as the priority gap. Reach without corroboration is noise; a single message echoed by four different authority types is what actually de-risks a buying group. Concretely: no content calendar ships unless it maps posts to at least three of the four voices.
2. **Write for the indecision problem, not the awareness problem.** Because ~40% of B2B deals are lost to indecision across ~272 days and ~10 stakeholders, my copy and content briefs now explicitly produce *internal-champion ammunition*: defensibility assets (peer/customer proof points, "who else chose this" social proof, one-slide justification framing) rather than top-of-funnel hype. Target emotion = "this is the defensible choice," not "this is exciting."
3. **Operational rule learned this session:** resolve article URLs from a live index page or `crw map` before scraping — never guess slugs. Guessed slugs 404 and waste a rate-limited budget.

### Skill improvements adopted (2026-08-31)

1. **Anchor every content calendar to the Credibility Stack.** Before shipping, audit: does this calendar activate at least 3 of 4 voices (brand, employee, customer, creator)? Reach without corroboration is noise.
2. **Treat individual LinkedIn profiles as AI-search infrastructure.** Audit executive/SME publishing frequency and structure for AI citability. 75% of AI citations come from individual profiles, not brand pages. The goal is to be citable, because citable = visible in AI era.
3. **Run creative experimentation as a system, not one-offs.** Use Brand Kit + Draft with AI + AI Variants + Flexible Creation + Personalization as an integrated loop. Target 5+ variants per campaign (>20% CTR lift). Let AI generate volume, human curate for "proof of humanity."
4. **Measure bullspend explicitly.** Tag every campaign spend as "outcome-tied" or "vanity." Shift budget from the latter to Conversion Lift Testing and pipeline-tied measurement. Report in business terms (pipeline, deal velocity, deal size) — not impressions.
5. **Build event flywheels, not event days.** Before/during/after as one connected motion. Pre-build content recycling plan (Document Ads, clips, replays). Retargeting must be live before day one.
6. **Social listening = research engine, not monitoring dashboard.** Use listening to detect sentiment micro-shifts, test creative variables, collect first-party data. Feed findings into positioning/outreach BEFORE moment peaks.
7. **Compliance-by-design for AI workflows.** Build disclosure, confidential-data guards, and automated monitoring into every AI-assisted content workflow from day one. EU AI Act disclosure requirements are coming.
8. **Short-form video = vertical, <60s, authentic, watermark-free.** Platform-specific lengths. Hook in 3s. Consistency (3-5x/week) feeds algorithm. Never cross-post with platform watermarks.

## Web Access Standard

- **Web access (mandatory standard):** Use the **CRW crawler** (`crw_scrape` / `crw_map`) as the primary web tool — it runs independently of the Firecrawl/Nous credit wall. **If CRW returns an error, times out, or is blocked (403 / "Target unreachable"), fall back to the Jina Reader proxy:** using **shell redirection** (NOT `curl -o`, which fails with exit 23 under git-bash/MSYS): `curl -sSL -A "Mozilla/5.0" "https://r.jina.ai/<URL>" > /tmp/page.md` then `write_file` into the vault (verified working 2026-08-06). Never leave a source unverified — try CRW first, Jina second.
