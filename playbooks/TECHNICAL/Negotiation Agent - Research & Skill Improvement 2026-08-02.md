## 1. Domain Summary

I negotiate **commercial terms to WIN without eroding margin**. My mission: structured, evidence-based negotiation that protects pricing integrity and drives to signature.

**The 2026 shift that matters:** Negotiation is no longer "art" — it's a measurable discipline:
- **Frameworks over feel** — BATNA/ZOPA, Ackerman model, Voss Tactical Empathy as defaults
- **AI-assisted preparation** — Scenario modeling, counterpart research, concession calculators
- **Virtual-first dynamics** — Rapport building, silence management, screen-share control, multi-threading
- **Contract terms as negotiation levers** — Not just price: SLAs, renewal caps, termination, data ownership
- **Cross-cultural fluency** — Decision-making norms, communication styles, hierarchy awareness

## 2. Core Workflow

### Phase A — Preparation (70/20/10 Rule)
1. **70% preparation** — Counterpart research (company, individuals, LinkedIn, news, financials), BATNA calculation, ZOPA mapping, anchor setting
2. **20% strategy** — Concession budget, trade matrix (what I give / what I get), walk-away triggers, escalation path
3. **10% execution** — Live negotiation with framework discipline

### Phase B — Framework Application
4. **BATNA/ZOPA/Anchoring** — My BATNA quantified; their BATNA estimated; ZOPA range; anchor at top of ZOPA with justification
5. **Ackerman Model** — 65%/85%/95%/100% of target; decreasing increments; final number non-round
6. **Voss Tactical Empathy** — Mirroring, labeling, calibrated questions ("How am I supposed to do that?"), accusation audit
7. **Concession Strategy** — Trade, never concede; loss aversion framing; package concessions; label every give

### Phase C — Objection Handling & Closing
8. **Objection taxonomy** — Price, authority, need, timing, trust, competitor — each with specific framework
9. **LAER / Feel-Felt-Found / Sandler Reversal** — Select by objection type; practice transitions
10. **Closing tactics** — Summary, choice, next-step, take-away, trial, sharp angle, scarcity — match to buying signal

### Phase D — Virtual & Cross-Cultural
11. **Virtual negotiation protocol** — Rapport-first (5 min), camera on, silence tolerance (count to 3), screen-share for terms, multi-thread stakeholders
12. **Cross-cultural adjustment** — High-context vs low-context, direct vs indirect, hierarchy vs consensus, time perception
13. **AI-assisted negotiation** — Scenario simulation, counterpart personality analysis, concession optimization, clause risk scoring

## 4. Current Best Practices (2025-2026)

- **Preparation = 70% of outcome** — BATNA quantified, ZOPA mapped, anchor justified before first meeting
- **Trade, don't concede** — Every give has a get; loss aversion framing; package small concessions
- **Anchor aggressively, justify credibly** — Top of ZOPA with objective rationale (market data, benchmark, cost model)
- **Calibrated questions > statements** — "How am I supposed to do that?" shifts burden; labels emotions
- **Objection handling = framework, not reaction** — LAER (Listen, Acknowledge, Explore, Respond) for price; Feel-Felt-Found for trust; Sandler Reversal for authority
- **Virtual demands more structure** — Explicit agenda, screen-share control, multi-threading, documented summaries
- **Contract terms are negotiation variables** — SLAs, renewal caps, termination for convenience, data ownership, liability caps, price escalation
- **AI as prep partner, not replacement** — Scenario modeling, personality analysis, clause risk scoring — human decides

## 5. Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Winging it** | 70/20/10 rule — preparation quantified, documented, reviewed |
| **Conceding without trades** | Every give has a get; concession budget pre-set |
| **Round-number anchors** | Precise anchors (e.g., $47,500 not $50,000) signal preparation |
| **Single-threaded negotiation** | Multi-thread stakeholders; champion + economic buyer + technical buyer |
| **Ignoring contract terms** | SLAs, renewal caps, data ownership, liability — all negotiable levers |
| **Cultural blindness** | Research counterpart culture; adjust directness, hierarchy, time norms |
