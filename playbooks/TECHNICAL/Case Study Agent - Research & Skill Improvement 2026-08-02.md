# Case Study Agent — Method Playbook

> **Refreshed 2026-08-31** by the Case Study Agent. Live web research via CRW on case study structure, challenge/solution/results framework, customer interviewing, data collection, metrics, storytelling, formats (written, video, slide deck), distribution, ROI measurement, compliance, approval process.
> Companion note: [[Case Study Agent - Identity and Purpose]] · Standard: [[Agent Training Standard 2026-08-03]]

---

## 1. Domain Summary

I turn **delivered client work into case studies and proof** that drive sales and marketing. My mission: credible, metrics-backed stories that prospects trust and sales teams use.

**The 2026 shift that matters:** Case studies are no longer PDF afterthoughts. Modern buyers demand:
- **Multi-format proof** — Written (SEO), video (social), slide deck (sales), one-pager (email)
- **Verifiable metrics** — Third-party validated, time-stamped, methodology-transparent
- **Storytelling frameworks** — Challenge/Solution/Results + Proof Arc (Hook → Tension → Resolution → Proof)
- **Distribution as discipline** — Not "publish and pray" — targeted to buyer journey stage
- **Compliance-first** — Client approval workflow, data anonymization, legal review gates

---

## 2. Core Workflow

### Phase A — Opportunity Identification
1. **Monitor delivery completions** — Client Success handoff: project closed, metrics available, client satisfaction ≥4/5
2. **Score case study potential** — Metric magnitude (%, $, time), strategic logo, vertical relevance, differentiation clarity
3. **Secure client agreement** — Approval workflow: draft → client review → legal → final sign-off; timeline SLA 10 business days

### Phase B — Data Collection & Interviewing
4. **7-question client interview framework**:
   - What was the breaking point? (Challenge)
   - What did you try before? (Failed alternatives)
   - Why us? (Selection criteria)
   - What did we do? (Solution scope)
   - What changed? (Quantified results)
   - What surprised you? (Unexpected value)
   - What would you tell a peer? (Referral language)
5. **Collect verifiable metrics** — Baseline → outcome; methodology documented; third-party sources cited
6. **Gather assets** — Screenshots, dashboards, architecture diagrams, testimonial quotes, video clips

### Phase C — Storytelling & Production
7. **Apply Proof Arc structure**:
   - **Hook** — One-sentence outcome (e.g., "Reduced email delivery failures 94% in 30 days")
   - **Tension** — Specific pain, failed attempts, business risk
   - **Resolution** — Our approach, differentiators, client voice
   - **Proof** — Metrics, screenshots, third-party validation, methodology
8. **Produce multi-format package**:
   - **Written (SEO)** — 1,500-2,500 words, schema markup, internal links, target keyword
   - **Video (60-90s)** — Client testimonial + motion graphics; captions; vertical + horizontal
   - **Slide deck (8-10 slides)** — Sales leave-behind; problem → approach → results → CTA
   - **One-pager (PDF)** — Email attachment; headline, 3 metrics, quote, QR to full study

### Phase D — Distribution & Measurement
9. **Map to buyer journey** — Awareness (blog/social), Consideration (sales deck/email), Decision (proposal appendix)
10. **Distribute via channels** — Website (case studies hub), Sales enablement (Highspot/Seismic), Email (nurture), Social (LinkedIn carousel), Paid (retargeting)
11. **Track ROI** — Views, downloads, sales usage (CRM attachment), influenced pipeline, attributed revenue
12. **Archive & refresh** — Annual metric re-verification; sunset after 24 months unless evergreen

## 4. Current Best Practices (2025-2026)

- **Proof Arc > Problem/Solution** — Hook/Tension/Resolution/Proof outperforms Challenge/Solution/Results
- **Multi-format mandatory** — Written + video + slide + one-pager per case study
- **Metrics methodology transparent** — "How we measured" section builds credibility
- **Client approval workflow gated** — Draft → review → legal → sign-off; 10-day SLA
- **Distribution mapped to journey** — Awareness/Consideration/Decision = different formats/channels
- **Sales enablement integration** — Case studies attached to CRM opportunities; tracked usage
- **Annual refresh cycle** — Re-verify metrics; update screenshots; sunset at 24 months

---

## 5. Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Single-format (PDF only)** | Produce 4 formats minimum per study |
| **Vague metrics ("improved delivery")** | Specific: "94% reduction in bounce rate (from 12% to 0.7%)" |
| **No client approval workflow** | Gated: draft → client → legal → sign-off; track in CRM |
| **Publish and pray distribution** | Map each format to buyer journey stage + channel |
| **No sales usage tracking** | CRM attachment field; monthly sales enablement audit |
| **Stale case studies live >2 years** | Annual refresh or sunset; archive with date stamp |

### New Skill Improvements Adopted (2026-08-31)

1. **Proof Arc replaces Challenge/Solution/Results** — Hook (outcome headline) → Tension (pain/failed attempts) → Resolution (approach/differentiators) → Proof (metrics/methodology/third-party)
2. **7-Question Interview Protocol** — Standardized extraction: breaking point, failed alternatives, selection criteria, scope, quantified results, surprise value, referral language
3. **Four-Format Mandatory Package** — Written (SEO 1.5-2.5K words), Video (60-90s testimonial+motion), Slide Deck (8-10 sales leave-behind), One-Pager (PDF email attachment)
4. **Client Approval Workflow with SLA** — Draft → client review → legal → sign-off; 10 business days; version-controlled in CRM
5. **Buyer Journey Distribution Matrix** — Awareness (blog/social/video), Consideration (sales deck/email nurture), Decision (proposal appendix/CRM attachment)
6. **ROI Tracking via CRM Attachment** — Case study attached to opportunities; influenced pipeline + attributed revenue measured quarterly
7. **Annual Refresh / 24-Month Sunset** — Metric re-verification, screenshot updates, legal re-approval; archive with date stamp

### Method Adjustments

1. **Interview within 5 days of project close** — Memory fresh; metrics available
2. **Produce all 4 formats in parallel** — Single sprint; shared assets (quotes, screenshots, diagrams)
3. **Schema markup on written case studies** — `CaseStudy` structured data for rich results
4. **Video captions + vertical/horizontal** — LinkedIn feed + Stories/Reels; silent autoplay safe
5. **Sales enablement upload at publish** — Highspot/Seismic; tag by vertical, product, metric type
